Master Tailor Advice

How To Take Your Measurements

1. Chest / Bust

Wrap the measuring tape horizontally around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape level under your armpits and across your shoulder blades.

2. Natural Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, typically located roughly 1 inch above your belly button. Keep one finger between the tape and your body for breathing comfort.

3. Shoulder Width

Measure across the curve of your upper back from the tip of your left shoulder bone straight across to the right shoulder bone.

4. Sleeve Length

With arm slightly bent at the elbow, measure from the point of your shoulder down along the outer arm to just past your wrist bone.

5. Trouser Inseam

Measure along the inside of your leg from the crotch point down to the top of your shoe heel where you want the trouser hem to break.

6. Neck Collar

Measure around the base of your neck where a shirt collar rests, inserting two fingers under the tape to guarantee a comfortable bespoke closure.
